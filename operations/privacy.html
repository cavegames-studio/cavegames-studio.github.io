Operations: Math Challenge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Operations: Math Challenge game for Android ("the game") uses. The game is made by CaVe Games ("we", "us").

In short
  • The game does not collect, send or share any personal data.
  • You do not need to create an account to play.
  • Your progress, best score and settings are saved on your device only.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works completely offline.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Game progress: your Challenge game in progress, so you can continue it later, and your best Challenge score.
  • Settings: your choice of light or dark theme.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes this data.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • We do not show ads and do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not run any servers, and we do not sell or share any data.

3. Permissions

The game does not request any special permissions. It does not use the internet.

4. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update the game, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive any personal information from this.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because the game does not collect any personal data, we do not hold any data about you. You can delete everything stored on your device by uninstalling the game. If you have any question, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.
